vm identity

What the guest reports about the machine it runs on. DMI strings, systemd-detect-virt and the chipset are overlaid inside the guest; CPUID and the clocksource cannot be changed this way. Saved to the VM, pushed to a running guest immediately, re-applied at every boot.

The nested replica honours the hypervisor-level keys once it runs the identity-patched QEMU (sudo appsandbox-replica qemu build): acpi_oem_id, acpi_oem_table_id, acpi_creator_id, smbios_manufacturer, drive_vendor, disk_model, cdrom_model, usb_vendor, smbios_vm_bit, CPUID 0x40000000, hypervisor CPU flag. It is redefined on every save and shows the new identity at its next boot.
