VM identity —

What the guest reports about the machine it runs on. DMI strings (sys_vendor, product_name, bios_vendor, chassis_vendor, ...), systemd-detect-virt and the chipset are overlaid inside the guest; CPUID and the clocksource cannot be changed this way. Saved to the VM, pushed to a running guest immediately, and re-applied at every boot.

Hypervisor-level keys are honoured by the nested VPS replica (appsandbox-replica in the guest) once it runs the identity-patched QEMU (sudo appsandbox-replica qemu build): acpi_oem_id, acpi_oem_table_id, acpi_creator_id, smbios_manufacturer, drive_vendor, disk_model, cdrom_model, usb_vendor, smbios_vm_bit, CPUID 0x40000000 and hypervisor CPU flag ("not set"). The replica is redefined on every save and shows the new identity at its next boot.
